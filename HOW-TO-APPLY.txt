HOW TO APPLY THIS UPDATE
1. Close the app window (terminal).
2. Copy everything inside this zip's 'lumina-studyhub' folder into your existing lumina-studyhub folder.
   Choose 'Replace the files'.
3. Do NOT delete anything. Your .env and workspace folder stay untouched.
4. Start the app again with start_studyhub.bat. No new packages are needed.
5. Optional: add the new video lines from .env.example to your own .env (all have safe defaults).
